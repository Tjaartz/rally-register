Rally Register

The asset management team's table tennis ladder. Log every match, track ratings and see who runs the table.

Open Rally Register → Opens on claude.ai. Sign in with your Energy Partners account.
Live link https://claude.ai/artifact/8998AXCxmqThWcRTNDrZYB

Before you start

  • You need a Claude account in the Energy Partners organisation.
  • If the page says you don't have access, ask the register owner to share it with you as a Contributor. Viewers can see the table but can't record results.
  • Bookmark the live link above so the register is one click away.

Record a match

  1. Add yourself under Players with your name and surname.
  2. In Record a match, pick both players and set how many games you played.
  3. Enter the score for each game. The winner is worked out for you.
  4. Select Save result. The leaderboard updates for everyone straight away.

The rules

  • Every game goes to 11. From 10–10 you need a 2-point lead.
  • Play as many games as you like. Most games won takes the match; a level score needs a decider.
  • Ratings start at 1000 and move after every match (Elo, K = 32). Beating a higher-rated player earns you more.
